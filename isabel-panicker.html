Bio

Isabel was born and raised in suburban Chicago, Illinois. She earned her Bachelor’s degree in Chemical Engineering from Purdue University in 2023. At Purdue, she worked with Dr. Rakesh Agrawal in the Solar Energy Research Group, where her research focused on the solution-based synthesis of multinary sulfides for photovoltaics. She also worked as a Quality Engineering Intern at Tesla, contributing to continuous improvement of the drive-unit manufacturing line.

Isabel joined the Jamali Lab in 2023. Her research focuses on nanoparticles and their non-equilibrium behavior using liquid-phase transmission electron microscopy. In her free time, she enjoys watching films, drawing, and painting.
